Mirror Realm — Privacy

Mirror Realm has no accounts and collects no personal data. Here is exactly what happens to data when you use it (see docs/13-security.md §7 for the canonical version).

Data Where it lives Retention
Your photo Processed in memory on the server, never stored 0 — discarded after the level is built
Levels you share Firestore levels/ 30 days
Levels you submit Firestore submissions/ 30 days or until promoted
Device hash Random 16 bytes in your browser; used only for rate limiting Clear site data to remove

We do not collect IP addresses, geolocation, or any identifying data.
